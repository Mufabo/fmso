Some of my AI interaction go way beyond the scope of a simple blog post.
Those are bundled together into books.

## Introduction to Terraform on Google Cloud

A hands-on book for absolute beginners that goes from "what is
Infrastructure as Code?" to running Terraform in CI/CD pipelines with
separate dev, staging and production environments on Google Cloud.
It has 23 lessons of about an hour each, every one ending with an
exercise and a solution. It covers the full HashiCorp Certified:
Terraform Associate (004) curriculum, and goes beyond it with testing,
policy as code, and CI/CD with GitHub Actions and Cloud Build.

[Read on GitHub](https://github.com/Mufabo/terraform-book) ·
[Latest release (EPUB)](https://github.com/Mufabo/terraform-book/releases/latest)

## Kubestronaut: a hands-on textbook

*Work in progress.* One book for all five certifications that make up Kubestronaut: KCNA,
KCSA, CKAD, CKA and CKS. The 39 chapters are organized by topic, not
by exam, and each one says which exams it serves. It's written for
readers who already know Linux and containers. Most chapters come with
a lab on a local kind cluster: one task at a time, an automated check
after each, and one command to reset. Each Part ends with a timed,
scored mock exam in the style of killer.sh, and every certification
has its own practice exam.

[Read on GitHub](https://github.com/Mufabo/kubestronaut) ·
[Latest release (EPUB)](https://github.com/Mufabo/kubestronaut/releases/latest)
