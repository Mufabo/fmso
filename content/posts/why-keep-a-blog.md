---
title: 'Why keep a blog at all'
date: 2025-11-18
tags: [writing]
---

Because your thinking deserves a better filing system than a chat history
and a folder of screenshots.

A blog is slow social media in the same way a garden is slow grocery
shopping — technically true, entirely beside the point. The point is the
practice. Writing something down for a stranger forces you to find out
whether you actually understand it, and most of the time you discover the
understanding was three-quarters finished. The last quarter happens in the
editor.

It also gives your ideas a permanent address. Links here won't be
paywalled, algorithmically buried, or deleted when a platform pivots.
Twenty years of personal sites have outlived every feed that was supposed
to replace them.

This post is a sample — replace it with your own reasons. You have some,
or you wouldn't have built this.
